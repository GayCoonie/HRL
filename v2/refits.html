Construction, provenance, and earlier work

The native-sRGB source atlas is normalized against each hue’s own black, D65 white, and actual sRGB vivid endpoint before path integration. It is not a full-gamut triangle squeezed or clipped into sRGB. Both native refits are fitted independently on that source chart; full-domain checkpoints remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

The regular-bicone metric is [L − R/2, √3·R·cos(H)/2, √3·R·sin(H)/2]. Native coordinates are continuous. Display RGB quantization and export precision are separate operations.
