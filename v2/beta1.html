What is fixed, what changed, and what the rulers mean
R is chromaticness, K=1−L and W=L−R. Level remains brilliance/inverse blackness, not Y or scalar GenSpace lightness. Black dilution, white dilution and neutral exchange are different operations. Each new candidate has one learned coefficient bank shared by gamut realizations.
The boundary-only control transports the existing full source chart at fixed relative Y and boundary-relative u′v′ position. It does not claim a new empirical hue fit. Native sRGB is unchanged by that geometric repair. The finite cone is exact for the stated tabulated/piecewise-linear spectral convention, not a claim of exact biological sensitivity.
Both learned fits use full 3D HelmLab GenSpace path and sheet diagnostics, retained-pair COMBVD, and a common-coordinate conditioning penalty. There is no Oklab target or new observer-rating dataset in this pass. Numerical regularity is not equivalent to visual preference; residual narrow-blue structure is included in the report.
All versions retain D65 and source/target-white luminance conversion. Above-ceiling input is scaled into the solid, not rejected. Imaginary input is mapped onto the selected physical boundary; native sRGB adds its explicit linear-channel mapping. The displayed full-gamut colors are sRGB approximations. Exported XYZ is not display-clipped.
