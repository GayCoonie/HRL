The balanced candidate favors smoother R/L paths. The metric-leaning alternative lowers COMBVD further, with less improvement in smoothness. Both were fixed before their new ColorBench evaluations. These are development fits, not an approved release.

The smoothness figures use Oklab as a numerical proxy, not human ratings: 48 interleaved hues, 288 Reach ramps and 288 Level ramps, 257 samples each. The first and last three steps are excluded from the reported ramp statistics. The same source solid and tests are used for every model.

Provenance and interpretation

Only full-profile R/L coupling was trained: three invertible layers with six hue harmonics, 195 coefficients. Source geometry, hue identity, magnitude continuation, source atlas, and neutral shift stayed fixed. A differentiable cached XYZ grid supplied a synthetic visual regularizer; the displayed pixels and recorded validation scores call the actual JavaScript runtime.

Five scored generation and sixteen measurement columns were evaluated, with clipping and strict-input policies reported separately. OSA-UCS, MacAdam1974, Xiao, and threshold/tolerance data were not used in this refit. Inherited hue-field training still applies. Hong threshold performance regresses; the change is not an across-the-board improvement.
