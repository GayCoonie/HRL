What the operation preserves

R = chromaticness · K = 1 − L · W = L − R
Add black: (R′, L′) = ((1−a)R, (1−a)L), preserving R:W.
Add white: (R′, L′) = ((1−a)R, a+(1−a)L), preserving R:K.
Neutral exchange: R′ = R, L′ = L+δ, transferring a share between W and K.

At fixed Reach, moving Level is not the same operation as diluting the whole colour with white. The white dilution also reduces Reach. Hue identity is held fixed in HRL; the corresponding XYZ path may curve.

Full-gamut preview pixels are explicitly clipped to sRGB. Statistics use original XYZ, never display-clipped pixels. GenSpace lightness and distance are diagnostics, not new definitions of HRL Level or observer-rated blackness and whiteness.

ZCAM is evidence, not a drop-in triangle

ZCAM Section 2 introduces conceptual blackness and whiteness through distances in lightness/chroma. Its actual fitted Equations 17–19 differ: at ideal neutral white Jz=100, Cz=0, its blackness correlate is 20, not zero. These open-ended appearance correlates cannot simply replace the three nonnegative HRL shares.
