# HRL v2 next-pass research record

## Frozen pre-registration — before new results

Question: can a bounded continuation of the existing shared tonal map improve the dark/high-Reach blue region and whole-sheet behavior while retaining Beta 1's observer-fit accuracy and geometric contracts?

H1: much of the extreme common-map condition tail is real derivative anisotropy. Prediction: analytic/autograd and step-converged numerical derivatives agree on the worst region. Falsifier: the alleged extreme disappears under analytic evaluation or stable stencil refinement.
H2: continuation from metric-b2 with its own accuracy/path guards can improve targeted roughness more efficiently than restarting from 0.12. Prediction: at least one frozen candidate reduces native blue bending or worst path risk by >=5%, with native retained and full weighted STRESS each <= baseline +0.15 and all four global direct-runtime path CV means <= baseline*1.03. Falsifier: no executed candidate meets these criteria. These thresholds are research selection rules, not invented user preference scores.
H3: any selected candidate preserves the one-bank map, anchors, legal triangular domain, inverse behavior, mapped-input contract and current shared hue labels. Falsifier: changed baseline hashes, contract failures or new nonfinite conversions.

Experiments:
E1 confirmatory baseline tracer: explicit-record factory identity, exact source hashes and cache populations, baseline scores, direct-runtime sample/preview.
E2 confirmatory derivative audit: same ordinary and extreme source points, analytic versus autograd and numerical derivatives at multiple legal step sizes; report both determinant and condition.
E3 exploratory continuation series: same source charts, boundary, hue family, neutral progression and seven invertible layers. Start at metric-b2. First two candidates emphasize (a) path/sheet quality with mild conditioning and (b) stronger conditioning with stronger accuracy guard. Full coefficients and traces retained. Exact commands and added objective terms logged before each run. At most four initial fits and eight total fits, each <=700 LBFGS iterations; <=45 minutes fitting wall time. Pivots are appended, never rewritten as confirmatory.
E4 confirmatory frozen-candidate validation: same native retained3331 and full3813 populations; native mapped3813 remains separate if evaluated. Exact JavaScript forward/inverse and direct GenSpace path/sheet measurements, selected hue previews including critical blues and whole-circle controls; frozen148 source-hash check.
E5 confirmatory independent review: reviewer distinct from implementers evaluates hypotheses and supported claims. Verdict confirmed, partially-confirmed, rejected, or inconclusive.

Stop on verified eligible comparison; otherwise close executed trials honestly at the declared budget and retain the baseline. Never convert unsuccessful trials into success or broaden training with reserved/new observer data without recording a separate scope decision. No new non-COMBVD observer dataset enters this pass. Full upstream ColorBench is optional follow-up after a candidate is frozen and its actual prerequisites are available; reused historic scores must not be called fresh runs.

Alternative considered: revise the full 3-D hue/source field immediately. Deferred from this bounded pass because it changes a different empirical layer; tonal continuation allows us to isolate current smoothness/conditioning trade-offs first. Existing fixed-stimulus native/full hue transport mismatch remains a separate diagnostic observation.
