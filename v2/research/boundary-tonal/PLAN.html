Fixed contract

Black dilution, white dilution and fixed-Reach neutral exchange remain distinct. One learned coefficient bank per candidate applies across gamut realizations. Keep the shared hue labels/ring and neutral progression; gamut changes supply geometry rather than another learned bank.

Ordinary import accepts finite above-white or imaginary inputs by the agreed mapping policy. No added intensity coordinate and no automatic above-white rejection. Nonfinite or malformed data remain errors. ColorBench uses mapped imports; strict domain checks are diagnostics, not its headline policy.
