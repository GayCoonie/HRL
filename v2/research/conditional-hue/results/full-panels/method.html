See 40 panels · Model hashes and panel SHA-256

Each full panel samples identical public HRL coordinates with R = x/(width − 1), L = 1 − y/(height − 1) + R/2, only where 0 ≤ R ≤ L ≤ 1. For each model, actual toXYZ output is converted to display sRGB. Linear sRGB channels outside [0,1] are counted before clipping for display. The model records are identical across native and full realizations; model source identities and immutable record hashes are in manifest.json. H117.5/122.5 and H270/275/280 are neighboring, untrained contact check angles; H182.5 is a control. Do not infer full-gamut properties from its clipped RGB image. COMBVD and numerical physical saturation/luminance checks are separate.

V4 coupled/contact records were fitted by the capacity team. A brighter yellow and stronger low-Reach blue in a sheet do not establish an overall improvement: both v4 banks remain exploratory and their retained COMBVD is worse than release baselines. These discrete samples do not certify all hues or the continuous Reach–Level domain.
