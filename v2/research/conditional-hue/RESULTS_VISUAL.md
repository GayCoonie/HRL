# Direct-runtime v4 blue/yellow contact inspection

The [native 3×5 comparison](results/native-panels/contact-focus.png) and [native shifted-hue 5×5 comparison](results/native-panels/contact-shifted.png) show Beta 1, frozen joint, v3 guarded, v4 coupled guarded, and v4 contact at the **same public H, Reach and Level**. The [full-domain 3×5 comparison](results/full-panels/contact-focus.png) and [full shifted-hue comparison](results/full-panels/contact-shifted.png) mark display-clipped pixel counts. Each folder contains an accessible [panel page](results/native-panels/index.html), exact 40 PNGs, `manifest.json` with all five record and source hashes, and alt-text sidecars. All 40 native panels have zero clipping. The full H273 contact panel clips **6,819/8,481** sampled pixels, so full-domain comparisons must use original XYZ.

The separate [independent numerical receipt](results/visual-contact-probes.json) calls model factories directly and records 31 public coordinates for **each** of five models in both gamuts, before sRGB display conversion. Y is physical relative luminance; `s` is the model's physical R/L; relative chroma is the frozen GenSpace opponent-plane distance from the same-Level gray divided by the same-Level vivid-edge distance.

On the full domain, H120 sample Y rises from v4 coupled **.58939** to contact **.66266**, below joint **.77548**. H273 sample relative chroma rises **.11155→.18674**, below Beta 1 **.26929**. The shifted blue samples mean **.09946→.17713**, near but below Beta 1 **.18944**. The visible contact sheets therefore brighten yellow and reduce the earlier v3/v4 washed-out blue region across the tested offsets. They do not reproduce joint's yellow brightness or Beta 1's native blue saturation. At the H182.5 control sample R=.1,L=.5, native relative chroma changes only **.08659→.08696** and Y **.17137→.17376** between v4 coupled and contact. There is no obvious panel-wide jump at the tested neighboring hue offsets, but these few panels cannot certify continuity between samples.

Both v4 banks are failed experiments under the retained direct-JS COMBVD development comparison: native weighted scores **38.47710→38.71512** and full **35.174→35.774** from coupled to contact (larger is worse). The contact bank does not qualify as a replacement for Beta 1 or frozen joint. Capacity team's broader fixed-Reach direct-runtime sweep also still finds a full-domain shifted-hue J retreat of **.02791** at H287.5, R=.05 for contact, outside the focused H270–280 panels. All findings concern finite samples and development data; no independent observer holdout is established.
