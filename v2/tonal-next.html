Reading the triangles and their limits

Reach is chromaticness. Level remains brilliance / inverse blackness: K = 1 − L and W = L − R. Every point satisfies 0 ≤ R ≤ L ≤ 1. Black dilution, white dilution and neutral exchange are different operations.

The triangle runs from white at the upper left to black at the lower left, with vivid at the right corner. The lower edge has R = L; its near-edge strip uses R = 0.96 L. Both sheets evaluate the same coordinates, with a shared coefficient bank across each candidate’s gamut realizations.

The full physical reference solid extends beyond sRGB. This screen preview clips out-of-display colors to sRGB; it cannot show the complete physical gamut. The reported relative D65 XYZ values retain the model output before display clipping. A clipping mask marks affected pixels and is not part of the color model.

This is a research comparison. The frozen Beta 1 definition remains the public release. Numerical regularity and eligibility do not establish a preferred appearance.
