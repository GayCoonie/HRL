Color & coordinates

R/L display scale 0–175.75. Native coordinates satisfy 0 ≤ R ≤ L ≤ 1. Public blackness = 1−L; neutral share = L−R; chromaticness = R. These are coordinates, not literal mixing proportions of three fixed lights.

Full-domain correspondence and new pseudo-primary layout
The native color converted to full-domain HRL preserves XYZ. Replaying its native H/R/L in the full domain instead preserves the gamut-relative address. These are different operations. No clipped full-domain triangle is substituted for the native preview.
The carrier's three full-drive vertices are at the endpoints of the declared polygon's purple closure and its maximum-y green vertex. Its colors are not display RGB. The physical boundary retains the documented 5 nm CIE 1931 approximation.
