What is being picked, exactly?

HRL is Hue, Reach, Level. This picker uses the repository’s createHRL({ gamut: 'srgb' }) model unchanged. The public library uses 0 ≤ R ≤ L ≤ 1; the 0–175.75 and percentage controls are display scales of those same coordinates. A 17,576-position axis has indices 0 through 17,575.

The triangle has equal-length sides. Its horizontal coordinate is (√3 / 2)R; its vertical coordinate is L − R/2. White is at the upper left, black at the lower left, and vivid at the right. The small composition bar shows R, L − R, and 1 − L—triangle weights, not an RGB mixing formula.

The background field is progressively sampled from the real model, then interpolated for display. Sliders and the field cursor move immediately; the swatch and copied RGB come from a direct reference conversion, never from a canvas pixel. A worker keeps those calculations off the page’s interaction thread. During a hue change, the field status identifies the older hue until the new preview arrives.

This small UI is sRGB-only. The library’s Rec.2020 support remains available through its API; it has not been silently squeezed into an sRGB preview. Nothing is sent to a third-party service. Saved swatches and the last selection stay in this browser when local storage is available.
